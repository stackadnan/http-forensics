# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to work out how HTTP actually behaves. Disagreements about results are expected and useful. Disrespect toward people is not.

## Expected behavior

- Argue from evidence. If you disagree with an observation, reproduce it and share what you saw, with versions.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when you are unsure.
- Accept corrections to your own cases without taking them personally.
- Give review feedback on the work, not the contributor.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability") and say that it is a conduct report, or contact the maintainer, [@stackadnan](https://github.com/stackadnan), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
